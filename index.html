Current operational portfolio

Science. Field. Systems. Decisions.

Where science meets implementation.

I work at the intersection of water science, climate resilience and spatial intelligence. The practical question underneath the work is simple: how can better evidence lead to better water-investment decisions?

I currently support water security, WASH, irrigation, basin planning, climate resilience and digital monitoring in Kenya and Somalia. The thread connecting research and operations is the same: turning complex water information into something people can act on.

Doctoral research

12 basins. 729 studies. One persistent question.

How confident are we in the evidence used to understand African water systems?

Doctoral work at Rhodes University examined uncertainty across global models, reanalysis, Earth observation and local data — and how those disagreements should be handled in data-scarce basins.
