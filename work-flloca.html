Kenya

FLLoCA — connecting locally led climate action with water security

Challenge

County climate-planning can under-weight water-resources evidence. Locally led climate action is stronger when WRM, climate-risk screening and water-security investment sit inside the same workflow.

My role

Integrate water-resources management and climate-resilient water considerations into county climate-planning, and serve as a technical bridge between FLLoCA and K-WASH.

Approach

Identify shared methodologies, tools, investment interfaces and implementation risks; then capture lessons from county planning and field work as reusable guidance.

Outputs

Guidance for embedding WRM and climate-risk screening into investment prioritisation, plus knowledge-management frameworks, templates and technical products.

What it enables

County climate investments that are more water-aware, and two programmes that can learn from each other instead of running in parallel.
