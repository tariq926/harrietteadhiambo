Kenya · 19 counties

K-WASH — climate intelligence for rural water investment

Challenge

Rural water investment needs more than infrastructure lists. Counties need a way to see climate hazard, water-resource context and vulnerability together before screening and sequencing investments.

My role

Technical review of County Water and Sanitation Services Investment Plans and Priority Investment Action Plans across 19 counties, plus development of climate-risk and water-investment tools.

Approach

Climate hazardDrought · flood · heat
Water contextGroundwater · surface water
ExposurePeople · infrastructure
RiskClimate & water
ScreeningInvestment priorities

Outputs

Climate Risk & Water Investment Dashboard; climate-proofing screening and retrofitting guidance; MUS/WASH+ decision-support tools; cross-county learning from technical clinics and field engagements.

What it enables

Clearer climate-resilient rural water investment — from county plans to programme-level guidance.
