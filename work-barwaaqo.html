Water resilience at scale

Challenge

Rural and urban water-resilience operations generate geotagged field data, infrastructure progress and basin evidence that only become useful when they can be seen, trusted and used for supervision and investment decisions.

My role

Lead development and operationalisation of the programme’s Geo-Enabling Monitoring System (GEMS), with additional water-resources, basin-planning, SLWMP and El Niño preparedness support.

Approach

FieldSites · photos · Kobo forms
DataValidation · integration
Spatial intelligenceGIS · risk layers
GEMSDashboards · visibility
DecisionsSupervision · planning

Outputs

Geo-enabled monitoring, basin diagnostics linking surface water, groundwater, climate, land use and degradation, State-Level Water and Land Management Planning frameworks, and spatial risk analysis for hydroclimatic hazards.

What it enables

Portfolio monitoring, implementation tracking and investment prioritisation that stay connected to place — from catchment evidence to programme decisions.
