Challenge

Irrigation investment becomes smarter only when monitoring infrastructure, digital platforms, data governance and institutional users are designed as one system rather than separate workstreams.

My role

NIIC Workstream Integrator — technical coordination and quality assurance across monitoring infrastructure, digital platforms, decision-support tools, capacity development and data governance, with government co-design across MoWSI, NIA, WRA and other data partners.

Approach

Keep the technical architecture coherent, translate the strategic roadmap into implementable requirements and sequencing, and coordinate user requirements so institutional ownership stays in the design.

Outputs

Workstream requirements, technical specifications, procurement packages, implementation monitoring and reporting to the Task Team and government counterparts.

What it enables

A national irrigation information centre that can actually support decisions — not just store datasets.
